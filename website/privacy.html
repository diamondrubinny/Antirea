Privacy & Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-05 · Draft — pending legal review.

This is the policy for the norea.rubinny.net marketing website only. The NoREA / Antirea software you self-host is governed by your own policies.

Who we are

This site is operated by Diamond Rubinny. For privacy questions, use the contact details you publish on your main site.

What this website collects

This is a static website. It sets no analytics or marketing cookies by default. It uses a small amount of local storage on your device for one strictly-necessary purpose: to remember your cookie-consent choice. That data stays in your browser and is not sent to us or any third party.

Standard web-server logs (e.g. IP address, user-agent, timestamp) may be processed by the hosting provider for security and reliability. If you enable analytics or marketing tools in the future, document them here and gate them behind consent (the site is already built to do this).

Cookies & similar technologies

Name Category Purpose Retention
norea_consent Essential Stores your cookie-consent choice (local storage). Until you clear it / change the policy version.
norea_lang Essential (a setting you chose) Remembers the language you chose (local storage). Until you clear it.
norea_a11y Essential (a setting you chose) Remembers your accessibility-toolbar settings (local storage). Removed when you press Reset. Until you clear it or press Reset.

Non-essential categories (functional, analytics, marketing) are off by default and load only with your consent. You can change or withdraw consent anytime: open cookie settings.

Legal bases (GDPR) & Israeli law

Where the EU GDPR applies, non-essential cookies rely on your consent; essential storage relies on legitimate interest / necessity to provide the service. Where the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law applies, we aim to collect the minimum necessary and to be transparent about it. This draft does not establish full legal compliance on its own — have it reviewed by a lawyer for your specific use.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of your personal data, and withdraw consent. Because this site stores consent only in your browser, you can exercise withdrawal directly via cookie settings or by clearing your browser storage.

Third parties

The site is hosted by a hosting provider and its source lives on GitHub. No third-party analytics, advertising, chat or social pixels are embedded by default. Add any you introduce to this policy and to the consent categories.
